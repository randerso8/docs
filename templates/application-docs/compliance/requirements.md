## Evidence boundaries

State whether each reference is application evidence, central governance evidence, or shared-platform evidence. Identify the exact coverage and the application's remaining integration responsibilities. Central approval cannot prove runtime behavior; an exception cannot be described as implemented compliance. A SHOULD deviation needs rationale and accountable review and cannot waive a separate MUST. Non-applicability cites an absent source condition, not inconvenience or missing implementation.

## Review and changes

Record actual review of dispositions and the assumptions that trigger reassessment. Revisit rows after changes to responsibilities, triggers, technology/support parameters, state, contracts, or the central pin. Preserve unresolved guidance and evidence gaps with owners and next actions.
